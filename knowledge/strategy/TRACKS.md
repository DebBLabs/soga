# CONTINUOUS TRACKS

These tracks represent the enduring responsibilities of the Embodied Governance Research Program.
Unlike research sprints, they continue throughout the life of the program and provide continuity across changing priorities.

---

# 1. Research Track

Purpose:
Advance the scientific understanding of execution-time governance for AI-mediated systems.

Responsibilities:
- Literature review
- Research questions and hypotheses
- Experimental design
- Architecture evolution
- Research publications
- Cross-disciplinary investigation

---

# 2. Standards Track

Purpose:
Contribute research findings to open standards and related communities.

Responsibilities:
- DIF
- AAuth / OAuth
- PIC
- IETF
- OIDF
- Kantara
- Other relevant standards organizations

---

# 3. Implementation Track

Purpose:
Develop and validate research prototypes.

Responsibilities:
- SOGA implementation
- Mission Builder
- Stage Gate Engine
- Capability Registry
- Runtime experimentation
- Embodied agent integration
- Demonstration environments

---

# 4. Funding Track

Purpose:
Sustain the research program through grants, fellowships, partnerships, and related opportunities.

Responsibilities:
- Funding opportunity identification
- Proposal development
- Fellowship applications
- Research partnerships
- Strategic collaborations

---

# 5. Living Laboratory & Outreach Track

Purpose:
Maintain the public expression of the Embodied Governance Research Program while validating research through real-world engagement.

Standing Responsibilities:
- Public program alignment (website, GitHub, presentations, biography)
- Research communications
- Demonstrations and living laboratory activities
- Community engagement
- External discoverability
- Educational and outreach materials
